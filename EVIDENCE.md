# Evidence and sources

This is an interpreted view of one Newcastle site, not an accurate reconstruction of every year.

- **Country:** Awabakal and Worimi connection to Country continues. The 1026 interface endpoint is not the beginning of First Nations history. Early unnamed figures, plain clothing, routes and activities are provisional interpretations. No particular people, ceremony or documented event is recreated, and no community endorsement is claimed. [City of Newcastle](https://newcastle.nsw.gov.au/explore/history-and-heritage/aboriginal-culture); [Miromaa cloak reference](https://awabakallanguage.org.au/possum-skin-cloak-workshop).
- **Fauna and vegetation:** Wallabies, bandicoots and dingo use regional references. Counts, habitat on this parcel, movements, tree growth and seasons are illustrative. [Glenrock State Conservation Area](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/visit-a-park/parks/glenrock-state-conservation-area); [Miromaa Awabakal dictionary](https://www.miromaa.org.au/images/AwabakalResources/Books/Awabakal_Community_Dictionary.pdf).
- **Earlier courthouse:** A courthouse occupied Hunter and Bolton Streets from 1838. Its shape and demolition sequence remain approximate. [The Lock-Up history](https://thelockup.org.au/history/).
- **Post Office construction:** 1900–1903, with an August 1903 opening. Assembly order and scaffolding are interpreted. [Heritage NSW](https://apps.environment.nsw.gov.au/dpcheritageapp/ViewHeritageItemDetails.aspx?ID=5051298); [Snowball archive catalogue](https://hunterlivinghistories.com/2023/11/14/ralph-snowball-archive/).
- **Architecture:** The mapped envelope and credited 2015/2019 photographs guide the model. Heights, depth, roof, rear elevation and ornament remain approximate. These photographs do not establish the physical condition in September 2026. Use Photo Comparison to inspect the mismatches. Camera matches are diagnostics, not survey validation.
- **Twentieth century:** Clothing, vehicles and movements are period-inspired, not reconstructions of particular people or events. [Museums Victoria clothing reference](https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/247068).
- **Future:** Neglect, collapse, wildlife return, extensive plant growth and optional tidal water after 2026 form an authored scenario. They are not structural, ecological or climate simulations, nor a forecast of Newcastle or the disappearance of its people.

Photo licences and mapped-data attribution are in [ATTRIBUTIONS.md](ATTRIBUTIONS.md). Evidence and interpretation labels also remain available in the interface, including when on-image labels are hidden.
